About the app

Its main purpose

The main purpose of our app is to give the ability to create a task of anything you desire
and send it to another page where you can observe, edit, delete, complete, or delete all tasks any time you like.
You are also able observe any tasks you have coming up on our built in calendar, with a pin to notify you on where it is.
